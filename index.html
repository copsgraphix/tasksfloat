macOS note: this app isn't notarized by Apple yet, so after opening the .dmg and dragging it to Applications, macOS may say it "is damaged and can't be opened." It isn't actually damaged — Gatekeeper is just rejecting an unsigned app. Fix it by opening Terminal and running: xattr -cr /Applications/Task Float.app then open it again normally.
